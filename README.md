## Report 1 — Cryptocurrency Research
**Topic:** Cryptocurrency as an Interdisciplinary 
Socio-Technical System

**Abstract:**
A structured 20-page academic research report 
examining cryptocurrency through five research 
dimensions — technical foundations, market 
analysis, behavioral economics, and regulatory 
policy. Critically reviews published academic 
papers and institutional reports including BIS 
policy reports and SSRN studies.

**Key Areas Covered:**
- Blockchain architecture and cryptographic 
  hash functions
- Proof-of-Work vs Proof-of-Stake consensus 
  mechanisms
- Smart contract functionality and risks
- Cryptocurrency market volatility analysis
- Behavioral economics — FOMO, scarcity 
  mindset, financial literacy
- Regulatory governance and AML compliance
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)
- Environmental sustainability of mining

**Research Methodology:**
- Structured 5-stage literature review
- Critical analysis of 12+ academic papers
- Comparative methodological analysis
- Cross-disciplinary synthesis

**Key Findings:**
- Blockchain introduces decentralized trust 
  but markets remain highly volatile
- Behavioral dynamics (FOMO, scarcity mindset) 
  amplify speculative cycles
- Institutional responses (CBDCs) show 
  adaptation rather than resistance
- Long-term viability depends on regulatory 
  clarity and public trust
